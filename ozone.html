Under the surface

It is one system, not six tools.

Every module writes to the same account record, so the call knows what the text said, and the route knows what the call booked.

01

One record per store

Machines, service history, contacts, consent, and every conversation in one place — not spread across a spreadsheet and somebody’s phone.

02

Consent-aware outreach

Who may be called, texted, or emailed, with quiet hours and opt-outs enforced before anything sends.

03

AI that hands off

The assistant books, answers, and drafts — then stops the moment a thread needs a person.

04

Dispatch with a memory

Skill, drive time, parts on the van, and the promise made on the phone all decide who gets the stop.
