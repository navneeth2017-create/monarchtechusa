01 · The problem

Printers print what you send.

Nobody catches these on purpose. They surface when a store calls, when a customer photographs the pouch, or when 5,000 units are already on the dock. Every one of them is checkable against a spec — so we check.

01

Wrong UPC

The barcode scans to a different product — or to nobody. Retail deductions, chargebacks and recalls live here.

02

Wrong net weight

The pouch says one number, the spec says another. Net weight is a promise to the customer and to the state.

03

Missing warning block

Required language absent, paraphrased, or in the wrong place. The most expensive sentence on the panel.

04

Unsubstantiated claims

"Clinically proven" with no study behind it. We check copy against your claim whitelist — and flag the rest.

05

Dieline drift

Artwork built on v4 dropped into a v5 template. Panels stop matching the die; the fold cuts through copy.

06

Spot color swap

PANTONE 485 C quietly becomes 485 U, and the shelf color you paid for isn't the shelf color you get.

02 · Two gates

One before it leaves. One before it prints.

Most label disasters are cooperation failures between three parties who never re-check each other's work. Preflight puts a gate on both sides of the printer.

  • Gate 1 — outbound artwork. Your design file against the locked spec before it ever reaches the printer.
  • Gate 2 — inbound printer proof. What the printer sends back, checked again. This is where versions drift and colors get substituted.
  • The spec is the referee. UPC, weights, warnings, claims, dieline version, spot colors — locked once, checked every time.
  • You approve, we record. Every verdict carries the person, the minute, and the file hash. Nothing ships on our word alone.

03 · The checklist

What gets checked. Every proof, every time.

The checklist runs the same way a careful human would — except it never gets tired on the fourth revision, and it remembers what version 2 said.

  • UPC / GTIN digits against the spec
  • Net weight, serving size + units
  • Ingredient panel present + ordered
  • Required warning language, verbatim
  • Claims against the approved whitelist
  • Dieline version + panel dimensions
  • Trim, bleed + safe areas
  • Spot colors vs named PANTONEs
  • Image resolution at print size
  • Barcode size + quiet zone
  • Lot / date code format
  • Fonts embedded + licensed

38 automated checks in the current build. The list grows with every proof we see — a mistake caught once becomes a check forever. Color on press and legal sign-off stay human, on purpose.

04 · What stays human

We flag. We don't certify.

Preflight is the QA layer between you and your printer — not a compliance authority, not a print shop. Here is the honest line.

What Preflight catches

Everything checkable against the spec.

  • Numbers that don't match: UPC, weight, counts
  • Language that's missing or paraphrased
  • Claims with nothing behind them
  • Versions, sizes and colors that drifted
  • Files that will disappoint the press

What stays human

Judgment, by design.

  • Color — a PDF can't show you ink on substrate
  • Legal + regulatory sign-off — your call, on record
  • Whether the claim should exist at all
  • The final yes — a person signs, we keep the receipt

06 · Questions

The honest answers.

What a serious buyer asks before handing over their packaging.

What do you need from us to start?

The proof file (PDF is fine) and whatever spec exists — even a messy one. If the spec lives in spreadsheets and email threads, the first engagement includes building and locking a clean one. After that, every check runs against it.

Do you check color?

No — and nobody honestly can from a PDF. We verify the spot color is specified correctly (right PANTONE, right library) and that it didn't change between versions. How ink lands on substrate is a press-proof conversation, and we'll tell you when you need one.

Is this compliance certification?

No. Preflight flags mismatches, missing language, and unsupported claims against your spec. Legal and regulatory sign-off stays with you and your regulators. What we give you is the record: who checked, what was found, and who approved.

How fast is a single proof check?

Same business day in most cases. The report includes the verdict, the annotated proof, and a drafted reply your printer can act on immediately.

Do you print or broker printing?

No. We're the QA layer between you and your printer. That independence is the point — the checker isn't the one being checked.

What if the printer disagrees with a finding?

Good — that's the gate working. Every finding cites the spec line it came from, so the conversation is about facts, not opinions. If the spec is wrong, we fix the spec and record the supersede.
