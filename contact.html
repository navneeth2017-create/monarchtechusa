Questions

Do you replace our current CRM?

Usually, yes — but not blindly. If there is a system your team genuinely uses and it has an API, we can integrate it. The goal is one place where the work happens, not another tab.

How long does the first build take?

The first phase is scoped in weeks, not quarters. A focused build typically maps in week one, builds over the next two to three weeks, and launches with the team in week four. Integrations and data access can change that, so we scope it honestly.

What does it cost?

One build to design, integrate, and launch the system, plus a monthly partnership to host, support, and improve it. We scope the build after the working session; the ozone proposal on this site shows the shape of the investment.

Is the AI voice cold-calling people?

No. Voice is consent-aware, with opt-outs, recordings, and live human handoff built in. We design the sequence around the rules that apply to your market and your offer.
