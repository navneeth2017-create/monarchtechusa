02 · Standard vs. custom

A real product underneath. Your business on top.

You should not pay to reinvent the parts that every growth system needs. You should pay to make the parts that are uniquely yours work properly.

Comes standard

The core system.

  • Local lead discovery and dedupe
  • AI voice, SMS, and email sequences
  • Pipeline, tasks, and conversation history
  • Consent, opt-outs, and recordings
  • Hosting, monitoring, and support

Built around you

The custom layer.

  • Your market definition and fit rules
  • Your stages, fields, and handoffs
  • Your scripts, offers, and follow-up cadence
  • Your routing and reporting views
  • Integrations with the tools you already run

03 · Who it fits

Built for operators who would rather sell than admin.

The system is a fit when the work is relationship-led, the market is local or regional, and the follow-up matters as much as the first call.

Good fit

Ozone & air purification

Commercial assessments, recurring service, and a service radius full of properties that need a conversation.

Good fit

Home services

Roofing, restoration, HVAC, pest, landscaping — anywhere a booked estimate is the real unit of growth.

Good fit

Commercial field sales

Reps who need a live book, a map, a next step, and a clean record before they walk in the door.

Probably not

Pure self-serve SaaS

If no one follows up, routes, or books a conversation, a custom growth system is more than you need.
