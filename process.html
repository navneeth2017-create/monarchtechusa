Process

From messy to running. Weeks, not quarters.

A focused first phase: map the market, build the system around the real work, and launch with the team that will use it.

The shape of it

We do not disappear into a discovery phase. We build in the open, with your team in the room and a working system at the end of each phase.

Map01 Build02 Launch03

Phase 01 · Map

Pin down the market.

We start with the market and the offer: the service area, the categories that fit, the questions your team asks, and the stages a real opportunity moves through. This is where we decide what the system should do — and what it should ignore.

  • Service area and ideal-fit categories
  • Stages, handoffs, and the first scripts
  • Data sources, access, and existing tools

Week 1

Inputs
  • Service area
  • Ideal fit and exclusions
  • Current stages and scripts
  • Data and access
Output

A one-page market map and the first sequence your team will run.

Phase 02 · Build

Build around the real work.

We configure the core CRM, lead finder, sequences, and pipeline, then extend the parts that are uniquely yours. Integrations, routing, reporting, permissions — built against the workflow we mapped, not a generic template.

  • CRM, data model, and lead engine
  • Voice, SMS, and email sequences
  • Integrations, views, and team permissions

Weeks 2–3

Inputs
  • Mapped workflow
  • Approved scripts and offers
  • Tool access and sample data
  • A decision-maker on call
Output

A working system in a staging workspace, ready for a team walkthrough.

Phase 03 · Launch

Launch with the people who use it.

We go live with the team, train the people who will use it every day, and watch what happens in the first real week. Then we tune the scripts, stages, and routing from what the data says — not what the demo suggested.

  • Team training and a clear operating rhythm
  • First-week monitoring and fixes
  • A next-step plan for the next iteration

Week 4

Inputs
  • Staging walkthrough
  • Team roles and permissions
  • Launch date and first campaign
  • Support channel
Output

A live workspace, a trained team, and a short list of what to improve next.

What it takes

A working system, not a handoff.

You do not need a product team. You need someone who knows the market, someone who knows the work, and a way to make decisions quickly.

From you

A decision-maker in the room.

Someone who can say yes to the market, the offer, the stages, and the scripts. We move fast when the person who owns the work is close to the build.

From you

The real version of the workflow.

Not the clean process from a training deck. The workarounds, the exceptions, and the “we always do it this way” parts are what make the system useful.

From us

A working system and a clear launch.

The configured CRM, lead engine, sequences, integrations, training, and a first-phase plan for what to improve after launch.

Ongoing

A partner for the next iteration.

Hosting, monitoring, support, campaign optimization, and a monthly roadmap so the system keeps improving after the first launch.
