05 · Private by design

Know exactly where the data goes.

Private does not mean pretending the cloud doesn't exist. It means drawing a clear boundary, enforcing it, and giving you a dedicated or on-prem option when you need full residency.

Inside your private boundary

The work and the context.

  • Prompts, documents, and embeddings
  • Model weights and custom adapters
  • Inference logs and evaluation sets
  • Call transcripts and summaries
  • Retrieval indexes and tenant namespaces

Vetted infrastructure

The services that stay outside.

  • App hosting — Railway
  • Billing — Stripe
  • Accounting — QuickBooks
  • Telephony — Telnyx or Twilio
  • Each under contract, access-controlled, and listed as a subprocessor

For full data residency, we deploy a dedicated instance or an on-prem appliance — same stack, same controls, inside your facility.

Questions

The honest answers.

The questions a serious buyer asks before moving AI into a private environment.

What does “private” actually mean here?

Inference and retrieval run on Monarch-controlled hardware. Your prompts, documents, embeddings, and model adapters stay inside your tenant boundary. App hosting, billing, and telephony are separate vetted services, listed as subprocessors. For full residency, we deploy dedicated or on-prem.

Do we need to own the hardware?

No. Start on shared private inference. Move to a dedicated instance or on-prem appliance when volume, isolation, or residency requires it.

Can we use our own models?

Yes. We are model-agnostic. Bring open-weight models, adapters, or an approved endpoint; we benchmark and pin what works.

What happens when the node goes down?

A cloud fallback keeps critical workflows alive. We route by task and policy, so a hardware issue degrades gracefully instead of stopping the business.

How do you stop one company's data leaking into another's?

Per-tenant keys, isolated retrieval namespaces, scoped prompts, and audit logs. The AI layer follows the same company boundary as the app.

How do you price it?

The build is scoped like any Monarch system; the private layer adds infrastructure and model operations. We quote the deployment tier and the monthly run rate after the working session.
